# The Fin-tastic Fashion Critic

**Genre:** Comedy | **Date:** 2026-08-30

### Characters
Barry: A quiet man who works from home.
Gilly: Barry's pet goldfish.
Mrs. Higgins: Barry's friendly, observant next-door neighbor.

### The Story
Barry lived a simple life. His small apartment was neat, his work was quiet, and his best friend was Gilly, a goldfish with bright orange scales. Gilly just swam. Mostly. Until one Tuesday. Barry was getting ready for an important video call. He pulled out a bright yellow shirt, one he thought was quite cheerful. He held it up, admiring it, then glanced at Gilly. The goldfish zipped to one corner of the tank, then quickly flicked its tail as if swatting away a fly. Barry blinked. “Too much?” he mumbled to the fish. He swapped it for a plain blue shirt. This time, Gilly just floated calmly, slowly fanning its fins. Barry felt a strange tickle of an idea.

The next morning, Barry decided to test his theory. He held up a striped t-shirt. Gilly zoomed back and forth, bumping the glass lightly. "Oh, busy pattern, then," Barry decided, pulling out a soft grey jumper. Gilly settled into a peaceful hover near a plastic plant. From then on, Gilly became Barry’s silent style advisor. Before dressing each day, Barry would hold up his chosen outfit to the tank. A quick tail flick meant 'no,' a calm float meant 'yes,' and a lazy swim in circles meant 'maybe, but think carefully.'

Barry’s wardrobe slowly changed. Out went the loud Hawaiian shirts and the slightly-too-tight jeans. In came muted colors, comfortable fabrics, and sensible cardigans. His colleagues on video calls started complimenting him. “Barry, you look very... professional today!” one said. Another noticed, “You always seem so put-together now.” Barry would just smile, thanking them, secretly giving Gilly all the credit. He even started talking to Gilly about his choices. “What about these new trousers, Gilly? Are they too casual for the grocery store?” Gilly would often just stare blankly, but Barry felt a connection. He once took Gilly to the pet store in a plastic bag to buy new flakes, and held up a sparkly new tie he saw. Gilly did a full 360-degree spin. “You love it!” Barry whispered, beaming, ignoring the strange looks from other customers.

Then came the grand event: a big online presentation for work. Barry needed to look his absolute best. He’d found a vintage velvet jacket, a deep, rich purple, and a matching silk scarf. This was it. He held them up to Gilly’s tank, excitement bubbling. Gilly, however, reacted with alarm. The little goldfish swam rapidly in circles, bumping the glass, looking quite distressed. It even seemed to make tiny, gasping motions. Barry’s face fell. “It’s *that* bad?” he whispered, crestfallen. He quickly changed into his usual plain blue shirt and a sensible brown cardigan. Gilly immediately calmed down, resuming its placid swim.

### Conclusion
Barry, feeling slightly deflated but utterly convinced of Gilly’s genius, decided to tell Mrs. Higgins, his next-door neighbor, all about his fish’s amazing fashion sense. Mrs. Higgins, a sweet but rather nosey older lady, listened intently. When Barry finished, she chuckled. Not a little polite chuckle, but a full-bodied, slightly mischievous laugh. “Oh, Barry, you and your Gilly,” she said, wiping a tear from her eye. “You really needed help with those loud shirts, dear. And I thought Gilly deserved a little more credit than just floating.” Barry stared at her, then back at Gilly, who was now just fanning its fins innocently. Mrs. Higgins then pointed a gnarled finger. “See that tiny little device stuck to the back of the tank, just below the water line? It’s a color-changing LED. I just used this tiny remote from my window.” She held up a remote control, no bigger than her thumb. Barry’s jaw dropped as he noticed a faint, almost invisible flicker of light from behind Gilly’s tank. He watched Gilly, now just a regular goldfish, and then looked at his sensible brown cardigan. He still looked put-together, though.
