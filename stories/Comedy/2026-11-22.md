# Arthur and the Gnome

**Genre:** Comedy | **Date:** 2026-11-22

### Characters
Arthur Pringle: A man who likes things neat and tidy, especially his front yard.
Mrs. Gable: A kind, elderly neighbor who lives next door.
Mr. Henderson: Another neighbor, a bit gruff but well-meaning.
Barnaby: A very ugly garden gnome.

### The Story
Arthur Pringle was a man who loved order. He had just moved into his new house on Elm Street, and his front lawn was a masterpiece. Every blade of grass stood at attention, perfectly trimmed. The flowerbeds were immaculate. Arthur spent hours making sure everything was just right. So, when he woke up one sunny Tuesday morning and saw it, his peaceful mood shattered. There, right in the middle of his perfect lawn, sat a garden gnome. It was a truly hideous thing, with a bright red hat, a long white beard, and a fishing rod. Arthur hated garden gnomes.

He frowned deeply. Clearly, one of the neighborhood kids was playing a prank. He picked up the gnome, walked to Mrs. Gable’s house next door, and placed it gently by her rose bushes. Mrs. Gable was known for her many garden decorations, so Arthur figured it would blend right in. Problem solved.

The next morning, Arthur opened his curtains. His jaw dropped. The gnome was back. Right there, in the exact spot on his lawn. This was no longer a simple prank. This was war. Arthur snatched up the gnome, drove it five miles to the nearest park, and left it under a big oak tree. He even gave it a little pat and a warning not to come back. As he drove home, he whistled, feeling quite pleased with himself.

But the gnome was a fighter. On Thursday morning, Barnaby, as Arthur had now secretly named him, was back. He sat there, fishing rod ready, as if he’d simply gone for a morning stroll. Arthur stared at him, bewildered. How? He hadn't seen anyone. He started to feel a shiver of fear. Was the gnome magic? Was someone watching him? He bought a cheap security camera and set it up by his window, pointing at the lawn.

Friday morning, Arthur checked the camera footage. He fast-forwarded through the night, past the sleepy cats and the rustling leaves. Then he saw it. Around seven in the morning, a familiar figure appeared. It was Mrs. Gable. She walked straight to Arthur's lawn, picked up Barnaby, and placed him back down with a cheerful little tap on his hat. Then she smiled and walked away. Arthur's face turned as red as Barnaby's hat. Mrs. Gable! The sweet old lady! He had been so wrong. He grabbed Barnaby, marched out the door, and stomped over to Mrs. Gable's house.

He found Mrs. Gable and Mr. Henderson, another neighbor, having tea on Mrs. Gable's porch. They looked up, smiling warmly. "Good morning, Arthur!" Mrs. Gable chirped. "Just in time for a cup."

Arthur held up the gnome. "Mrs. Gable," he said, trying to keep his voice even, "can you tell me why you keep putting this... this *thing* back on my lawn?"

### Conclusion
Mrs. Gable looked at the gnome, then at Arthur, and then she giggled. Mr. Henderson chuckled too. "Oh, Arthur," Mrs. Gable explained, "you silly goose! It's Barnaby! It's your week to host him. He's the neighborhood watch gnome. Whoever has Barnaby on their lawn is the 'eyes and ears' for the street for seven days. We just dropped him off on Tuesday to start your shift, but he keeps vanishing! We thought you were playing a game!" Arthur stared at Barnaby, then at his smiling neighbors, then back at Barnaby. The hideous gnome now seemed to wink at him. It was *his* week.
