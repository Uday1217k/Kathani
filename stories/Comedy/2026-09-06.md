# The Great Fern Scare

**Genre:** Comedy | **Date:** 2026-09-06

### Characters
*   Mildred: An elderly lady who loves plants, a bit hard of hearing.
*   Gary: The delivery driver, prone to worrying.
*   Mrs. Henderson: Mildred's neighbor, very curious and easily panicked.
*   Officer Miller: A local police officer.

### The Story
Mildred hummed a little tune as she waited by her front door. Her new plant was due today. Not just any plant, mind you. This was a ‘Sunset Whisper Fern’, a rare beauty she’d ordered from an online nursery. The website said it changed colors with the evening light. Mildred imagined it sitting proudly on her windowsill, glowing softly.

A big brown truck pulled up. Gary, the delivery driver, hopped out. He looked pale. In his hands, he held a cardboard box, about the size of a small dog kennel. On the box, big red letters screamed: "CAUTION: EXTREME CONTENTS – HANDLE WITH UTMOST CARE – DO NOT JAR.” Below that, a smaller sticker read: "Special Material. Read Inner Label BEFORE Opening." Gary swallowed hard, his eyes wide.

"Mildred," he called, his voice shaky as he came to the porch. "Got a package for you. It's... uh... it's got some serious warnings on it."

Mildred cupped her ear. "Warnings, dear? About what? Is it fragile?"

Gary leaned in. "It says 'Extreme Contents' and 'Do Not Jar'! What exactly is in here, Mildred?" His eyes darted nervously to the box. He’d delivered everything from tiny screws to refrigerators, but this box had him truly spooked.

Mildred chuckled. "Oh, that's just my new fern, dear. The Sunset Whisper. It’s a very delicate plant. They probably just don't want it shaken up too much." She signed the digital pad, smiling sweetly.

Gary left, still looking over his shoulder at the box on Mildred's porch. As he drove off, he saw Mrs. Henderson, Mildred’s next-door neighbor, peeking from behind her curtains. Mrs. Henderson had hawk-like vision and a gift for making small things seem very, very big.

It wasn't five minutes before Mrs. Henderson was on the phone. "You won't believe what Mildred just received! A huge box, Gary the delivery driver looked absolutely terrified, and it's covered in warnings! 'Extreme Contents'! 'Do Not Jar'! I think she's ordered something... dangerous!"

The phone calls spread faster than a sneeze in a crowded room. Soon, Mr. Peterson from down the street was telling Mrs. Higgins that Mildred had a box marked "Hazardous Material, Do Not Approach." Mrs. Higgins told her nephew, who worked at the local paper, that Mildred had a container of "unknown, highly unstable, possibly exploding contents."

By late afternoon, a small crowd had gathered on the pavement outside Mildred's house. People whispered, pointed, and took shaky phone pictures with their cell phones. Officer Miller arrived in his patrol car, looking serious. He walked up to Mildred's porch, where the box still sat, waiting to be opened. Mildred, inside, was making herself a cup of tea, quite unaware of the commotion.

Officer Miller knocked gently. Mildred opened the door, squinting at the bright sunlight. "Oh, hello, Officer. Lovely day, isn't it?"

"Mildred," Officer Miller said calmly, "we've had a few calls about this package." He pointed to the ominous box. "Can you tell me what's inside?"

"Oh, that?" Mildred beamed. "That's my Sunset Whisper Fern! It just arrived. I was just about to open it. Would you like to see it?" Without waiting for an answer, Mildred knelt down, carefully peeled back the tape, and opened the cardboard flaps.

Inside, nestled in soft white packing peanuts, sat a perfectly ordinary, small fern. Its leaves were a lovely green, delicately tinged with purple. It looked very fragile, very pretty, and utterly harmless.

The crowd outside gasped, not in fear, but in utter confusion.

### Conclusion
Mildred gently lifted the fern out of the box. She looked up at Officer Miller, then at the stunned faces of her neighbors. "See?" she said, holding up the plant. "Just a lovely plant." She then pulled out a small, folded paper from the box. "Oh, and here's the inner label Gary mentioned." She put on her reading glasses. "It says here: 'CAUTION: EXTREME CONTENTS - DO NOT JAR' actually means, 'The plant inside is extremely delicate. Do not shake the box or allow it to be dropped, as this plant is very sensitive to movement, much like a glass jar.' Oh, and 'Special Material' refers to the unique soil mix. Silly, isn't it? All that fuss for a sensitive little fern."

The crowd stared, then a few people started to chuckle, then everyone else joined in. Officer Miller rubbed his temples, a faint smile playing on his lips. Mrs. Henderson turned bright red and quickly walked back to her own house. Mildred, completely oblivious to the drama she had just caused, simply went inside to find a nice, quiet spot for her very delicate, very harmless Sunset Whisper Fern.
