# The Daily Notes

**Genre:** Romance | **Date:** 2026-08-12

### Characters
Alex: A precise potter who loves order.
Jamie: A lively painter who embraces creative chaos.

### The Story
Alex loved the quiet morning hours at the community art studio. It was a place for peace, for shaping clay into perfect forms. But there was one problem: "The Other Artist." This person always used the studio in the afternoons or evenings, leaving behind a trail of creative messes. Paint splatters near the shared sink, clay dust on Alex's wheel, brushes left soaking for days.

Alex, a fan of neatness, started leaving notes. The first one was simple, taped to a half-empty tube of bright blue paint: "Please clean your brushes after use. – A."

A few days later, a new note appeared next to it, written on a torn piece of canvas: "They're soaking! It helps keep them soft. Relax a little, A. – J."

Alex rolled their eyes. "J," huh? The notes continued. Alex would complain about a stray palette knife, Jamie would retort with a playful drawing of a messy art studio, complete with tiny, angry stick figures. Alex mentioned the clay dust. Jamie left a little doodle of a dust cloud with a smiley face and a tiny, polite "My apologies, A. Your pottery is very neat, though. – J."

Slowly, the notes changed. Alex, despite the annoyance, found themselves looking forward to J's replies. Jamie’s cheerful handwriting and quick wit were infectious. Alex even started to notice the beauty in Jamie’s unfinished paintings, the bold strokes, the fearless use of color. "Borrowed your blue paint," one note from J said, "Ran out. Hope you don't mind. I'll replace it. – J." Alex wrote back, "I do mind. But I saw the painting you used it for. It was good. – A."

Jamie's next note was on a small piece of paper, tucked into Alex's favorite pottery tool kit. "Your pottery piece today, the one that looked like a perfect wave? How do you do that? It's really beautiful. – J."

Alex felt a blush warm their cheeks. They found themselves smiling more in the studio, even if "The Other Artist" wasn't there. They started to wonder about J. Were they tall or short? Did they have messy hair or a neat ponytail? Did they laugh a lot? Alex left a note, "Practice. And not leaving tools scattered. See you around? – A."

Jamie replied, "Maybe! I'm usually here evenings. You're a morning person? – J."

Alex took a deep breath. This was it. "Yes. Early bird. How about we grab coffee outside the studio? Say, Saturday, 10 AM, The Daily Grind? – A."

A bold, colorful note greeted Alex the next morning: "Sounds good! Look for the person holding a very bright, slightly paint-stained scarf. – J."

Alex felt a flutter in their chest. They wrote back, "I'll be holding a small, perfectly shaped ceramic mug. – A."

### Conclusion
Saturday morning, Alex walked into The Daily Grind, holding their special mug. The coffee shop was bustling. Alex scanned the room, looking for a splash of color. And there, sitting at a table near the window, was someone with a vibrant, paint-stained scarf draped over their shoulders. Alex’s heart skipped a beat. They started walking towards the table, a nervous smile forming. As Alex got closer, the person looked up. It was Jamie, their roommate, who was currently sipping coffee from a very familiar, perfectly shaped ceramic mug.

Their eyes met, wide with shock. "Alex? Jamie?" they both said at the same time.

"You're the other artist?" Jamie gasped, pointing at Alex.

"You're 'A'?" Alex asked, pointing at Jamie.

"You're 'J'?" Jamie replied, a slow smile spreading across their face.

Then, a sudden, shared realization hit them. They burst out laughing. "Wait," Jamie said, wiping a tear from their eye. "You live at 12B, too, right?"

"Yeah," Alex nodded, still laughing. "And you use the studio?"

"Every evening after work!" Jamie exclaimed. "And you're the morning person I always miss?"

They both shook their heads, still chuckling. All those notes, all that imagining, all that anonymous longing – for the person who shared their kitchen. It was the most wonderfully messy, perfectly neat surprise.
