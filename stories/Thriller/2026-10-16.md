# The Reset Button

**Genre:** Thriller | **Date:** 2026-10-16

### Characters
Arthur Finch: The main character, who wakes up with no memory.
Dr. Elena Petrova: The calm, unseen voice guiding Arthur.

### The Story
Arthur woke up on a plain bed. The room was stark white, without a single window. There was one door, also white, blending into the wall. He sat up fast, a jolt of panic going through him. Where was he? How did he get here? His head felt fuzzy, like he’d slept for a hundred years. He tried to remember anything – his name, his home, the day before. Nothing. It was all a blank, empty space.

He slid off the bed. The floor was cold. He walked to the door and pushed the handle. It didn't turn. Locked. He pounded on it. "Hello? Is anyone out there?" His voice sounded strange, rough. No answer. He felt a deep, chilling fear settle in his gut. Was this a trick? A kidnapping?

Suddenly, a small, silver grate on the wall lit up. A calm, female voice came through it. "Good morning, Arthur. Please stay calm. You are safe."

Arthur jumped back. "Who is this? Where am I? Let me out!"

"My name is Dr. Elena Petrova," the voice said, still calm. "You are in a recovery unit. You chose to be here. Everything is fine."

"I chose to be here?" Arthur laughed, a harsh, humorless sound. "I don't remember choosing anything. I don't remember anything at all! This is a trick. Let me out, now!" He hit the door again, harder this time. His knuckles stung.

Dr. Petrova paused. "We understand you feel confused. This is a common effect of the procedure. Your memories will slowly return, but only the ones you need. The ones you wanted erased are gone for good."

"Erased? What are you talking about?" Arthur spun around, looking for cameras, for anything. There was nothing. Just white walls, a bed, a small table, and the speaker. He saw a tiny air vent near the ceiling. Too small. He looked under the bed, hoping for some clue, a tool, anything. His hand brushed against something cold and hard at the back. He pulled it out.

It was a small, wooden music box. Old and simple, with a tarnished brass key on the side. It looked familiar, but he couldn't place why. He turned the key. A gentle, tinkling tune began to play, soft and clear in the quiet room.

As the music filled the space, a flicker of an image hit him. Not of a kidnapping, but of a frantic, desperate meeting. A choice. A terrible, heavy burden he wanted to be rid of. His breath hitched. He wasn’t being held against his will. He remembered the feeling, not the facts. The feeling of wanting to disappear, to start over.

The music box played on. Another image, faster this time. A flash of anger, a raised voice, a mistake he could never take back. He didn't see details, just the raw, bitter emotion. He felt a wave of nausea.

### Conclusion
Dr. Petrova’s voice came back through the speaker, her tone still gentle, but with an edge of firm authority. "Arthur, please. That music box was left as a failsafe, a final trigger should you need it. We wiped your memory of the incident, yes, but not of *who you are*. You came to us because you did something very wrong. Something you couldn't live with. This room isn't a prison, Arthur. It's a fresh start, a chance to rebuild your life without the crushing weight of your past actions. You begged us for this. The 'tangy flavor' you taste, Arthur, is not fear of your captors. It's the bitter tang of the person you once were, slowly trying to remember what you paid so dearly to forget." The music box stopped, the last note hanging in the air. Arthur Finch stood frozen, the heavy truth settling around him like cold chains. He had locked himself in this room. He was the monster.
