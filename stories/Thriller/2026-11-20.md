# The Elephant in the Room

**Genre:** Thriller | **Date:** 2026-11-20

### Characters
Leo: A man who likes things orderly and lives alone.
Mrs. Gable: Leo's elderly neighbor.

### The Story
Leo liked his apartment. He liked it very much. Everything had its place. The magazines stacked just so, the remote control perfectly aligned with the edge of the coffee table, his shoes in a neat row by the door. His life was simple, and he liked it that way.

Then, things started to change. Small things, at first. A coffee mug on the counter when he was sure he’d put it in the dishwasher. A light left on in the bathroom. He blamed himself. "Must be tired," he’d mumble.

But it got worse. One morning, he found a different brand of milk in his fridge. A kind he never bought. Another day, a strange, floral scent hung in the air, not his usual lemon cleaner. He felt a chill. Someone was coming into his home. Someone was using his things. But why? Nothing was stolen. No windows broken, no forced entry.

He felt watched. Every shadow seemed to move. He checked the locks three times before bed. He woke up often, heart thumping, thinking he heard a floorboard creak. He told the building manager, Mr. Henderson, about it. Mr. Henderson checked the locks, checked the windows. Found nothing. "Maybe you're just stressed, Leo," he said, too kindly.

Leo knew he wasn't stressed. He was scared. He bought a small, hidden camera online. He stuck it to the top of his bookshelf, pointing towards the living room and his front door. He set it to record movement. "Now I'll know," he whispered to the empty room.

He went to work the next day, a knot of worry in his stomach. All day, he thought about the camera. What would it show? A thief? A ghost? He rushed home, barely took off his coat, and went straight to the camera. He plugged it into his laptop, his hands shaking slightly.

The video started. The screen was still, showing his empty living room. Then, the front door opened. Leo gasped. A man stepped inside. The man looked around, then took off his shoes, placing them in a neat row. He set his keys on the small table. He walked into the kitchen, opened the fridge, took out the milk. The milk Leo swore wasn't his. The man poured cereal into a bowl. He ate it. He moved a book on the shelf, straightened a rug. Everything Leo had blamed on the 'intruder'.

Leo stared, his blood running cold. This wasn't right. He didn't remember doing any of this. He looked closer at the man. His jaw dropped. The man... the man was him. It was *Leo*.

He watched the footage again, trying to make sense of it. He saw himself, moving around, doing all the things he thought an intruder was doing. But why didn't he remember? Was he sleepwalking? Was he losing his mind?

### Conclusion
On the fourth viewing, his eyes caught something small in the bottom left corner of the screen, just as 'he' walked into the kitchen. A small, green ceramic elephant, sitting on a tiny shelf by the hallway. A gift from his Aunt Sarah. He *knew* that elephant. It was on *his* shelf.

No, wait. *His* elephant had a small chip on its left ear. This one was perfect. And then, above the small elephant, a framed photo he'd never seen before. A woman with short red hair, smiling.

Leo's gaze shot to the front door visible in the footage. Above the doorknob, clear as day, was a small, silver number plate. *403*.

Leo lived in apartment *402*.
