# The Midnight Retrieval

**Genre:** Action | **Date:** 2026-11-07

### Characters
Leo: A serious night security guard at the city museum.
Maya: A museum assistant, usually a bit rushed.

### The Story
The Grand City Museum was a quiet place after dark. Leo, the night guard, usually spent his shift walking the calm halls, checking locks, and sipping lukewarm coffee from his thermos. Tonight was no different. He walked past sleeping dinosaurs and ancient pottery, the only sound the soft padding of his shoes.

Then, a red light blinked on his watch. Main gallery, section B. Motion sensor tripped. Leo’s heart gave a jump. He stopped dead. He pulled out his small radio. "Central, this is Leo. Got a trigger in main gallery, B." A crackle answered, telling him to check it out.

Leo clicked on his heavy flashlight, the beam cutting a thick line through the gloom. He moved fast, but quietly, hugging the shadows. He knew these halls better than his own small apartment. When he peered around the corner into the main gallery, he saw it: a quick, dark shape darting behind the massive statue of a pharaoh.

"Hey!" Leo yelled. The shape froze, then bolted.
This wasn't a cat, or a loose drape. This was a person.
Leo broke into a run. "Stop! Security!"

The figure was fast, weaving through the tall display cases. Leo followed, his breath coming in short bursts. He saw them duck into the ancient Egypt wing. That wing had fewer open spaces, more dead ends. Good. Leo grinned grimly. This was his chance.

He burst into the Egyptian room, his flashlight sweeping wildly. The room was full of sarcophagi and old gold jewelry, lit by the moon through high windows. He spotted the figure near the famous "Eye of Khepri" exhibit – a sparkling, fist-sized gem that sat under thick glass. The figure was small, dressed in dark clothes, and was clearly trying to pry something open or reach behind the case.

"Hands up! Don't move!" Leo yelled, aiming his flashlight beam directly at them. The figure froze, caught. Leo could see a small, dark backpack on their shoulders. A professional, then. After the Eye of Khepri, no doubt. This gem was worth a fortune.

The figure slowly turned around. It wasn't a burly thief with a crowbar. It was Maya, the museum assistant from the archives. She looked tired, her dark hair messy. And she wasn't holding a tool. She was holding a tiny, ordinary silver USB stick.

"Leo?" she whispered, her eyes wide. "What are you doing here?"
"What am *I* doing here?" Leo panted, still ready to pounce. "Maya, what are *you* doing? You broke in! What's with the backpack? Are you stealing the Eye?"

Maya looked down at the gem, then at her USB stick. She let out a small, tired laugh. "Stealing? No, Leo. My thesis data, all my research, it was on this. I was working late yesterday, and when I was packing up, it must have slipped from my pocket. It fell right behind this display case. I saw it go. I tried to get it, but couldn't reach it without moving the whole heavy case, and I knew I'd set off alarms."

She gestured behind the glass. "I couldn't just leave it. My whole career is on this thing! I figured if I came back tonight, quietly, I could just reach my arm down from the side, grab it, and be gone before anyone knew. I didn't mean to trip your sensors, I was being so careful!"

### Conclusion
Leo lowered his flashlight slowly. He stared at the priceless gem, then at Maya’s very common USB stick. He looked at her serious, worried face. He rubbed his temples. "You broke into a heavily guarded museum... for a USB stick?" Maya nodded sheepishly. "My life's work," she repeated. Leo sighed, half relieved, half utterly exasperated. "Central," he radioed, a note of disbelief in his voice. "False alarm. Just a very, very determined staff member retrieving some... personal property." He shook his head, a small, knowing smile finally spreading across his face.
