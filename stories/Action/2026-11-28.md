# The Sweetest Drop

**Genre:** Action | **Date:** 2026-11-28

### Characters
*   Lena: A skilled, quiet courier.
*   Mr. Finch: Lena's old, wise contact, who gives her missions.
*   Mia: A young girl, the true recipient of the package.
*   The Pursuers: Trained agents, part of the mission's setup.

### The Story
The rain fell hard, washing the city streets clean. Lena sat in her small apartment, cleaning her pistol. Her phone buzzed. It was Mr. Finch. "Job for you, Lena," he said, his voice a low rumble. "Small box. Top priority. Don't lose it. Deliver it to the old bakery on Elm Street. Midnight. Code word 'Sugar Plum'."

Lena went to the pickup spot. A gloved hand pushed a small, metal box across a dark table. It was heavier than it looked. "Handle with care," a voice said from the shadows. "It's vital." Lena nodded, tucked the box into her jacket, and walked out.

She drove her old car, cutting through traffic. A black sedan appeared behind her, moving fast. Lena gripped the wheel, her eyes scanning mirrors. The sedan got closer, then tried to block her. Lena swerved, hit the gas, and took a sharp turn into a narrow alley. Tires screeched. She heard a bang against the side of her car. She kept driving, pushing the old engine, until she lost them in the maze of backstreets.

She ditched the car a few blocks away and went on foot. The rain had stopped. She moved through shadows, her senses sharp. Two men stepped out from behind a dumpster. They were big, serious. "Hand it over," one said, his voice flat. Lena didn't say a word. She moved first, a quick punch to the chin of the closest man. He staggered back. The other one grabbed her arm. Lena twisted, used his own force against him, and threw him into the first man. They fell in a heap. Lena ran, not looking back. Her side ached, but the box was safe.

She reached the old bakery on Elm Street just before midnight. It was dark and quiet. The door was unlocked. She stepped inside. The air smelled of old bread and dust. A small light came from the back room. Lena walked toward it, her hand on her weapon.

A little girl sat on a stool in the back, drawing in a notebook. She looked up, her eyes wide. Lena looked around. No one else. "Sugar Plum?" Lena asked, holding out the box. The girl nodded slowly, a tiny smile forming on her face. Lena opened the box. Inside, nestled on soft cotton, were three colorful lollipops. Cherry, orange, and grape.

### Conclusion
Lena stared at the lollipops, then at the girl. Just then, Mr. Finch walked in from a back door, a soft smile on his face. The two men Lena fought, bruised but okay, followed him. "You passed, Lena," Mr. Finch said simply. "This was a test. Mia, here, she's been very brave, and these are her favorite. They're hard to find now. Your last two missions were about delivering fake intel. This one, about delivering something truly important to a truly important person, was the real test of your heart. And you, Lena, you have a very good heart." Lena looked at the lollipops, then at the girl's happy face, a strange, sweet feeling washing over her.
