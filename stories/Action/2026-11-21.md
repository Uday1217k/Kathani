# The Biscuit Breakout

**Genre:** Action | **Date:** 2026-11-21

### Characters
Anya: Main character, quick and brave.
Maya: Anya's younger sister, who loves a special kind of biscuit.
Guard Captain Thorne: Head of security, tough and serious.
Various Guards: Thorne's team, mostly big and slow.

### The Story
Anya stood across the street from Zenith Tower. It was a huge, shiny building, all glass and steel. Most people saw a company office; Anya saw a fortress. Her mission was simple: get inside, grab a small box from the 42nd floor, and get out. Nobody said it would be easy.

She slipped in through a delivery entrance, moving like a shadow. Inside, the air was cool and quiet. Cameras watched every corner, but Anya knew where they couldn't see. She climbed service stairs, her breath even, her mind clear. On the 30th floor, she had to cross an open hallway. A guard sat at a desk, looking bored. Anya moved quickly, low to the ground, past his blind spot. He never even looked up.

She reached the 42nd floor. It was a fancy office area, empty at this late hour. The small office she needed was at the end of the hall. She picked the lock fast. Inside, on a clean, empty desk, sat the box. It was plain white, no labels. Anya reached for it.

Just then, a voice boomed from the doorway. "Freeze!"

Guard Captain Thorne stood there, a big man with a hard face. He had a radio to his mouth. "Intruder on 42, office E! Block all exits!"

Anya didn't freeze. She grabbed the box, spun around, and ran. Thorne lunged, but Anya was too fast. She dodged his hand and burst out of the office. More guards were already coming down the hall.

"She has the package!" Thorne yelled.

Anya saw a cleaning cart. She pushed it hard, sending it rolling down the hallway, right into the path of two guards. They stumbled. Anya used the distraction to jump onto a window cleaner's platform, which was parked near a huge window. She hit the 'down' button. The platform hummed, slowly carrying her lower.

Thorne reached the window. "Don't let her get away! Shoot the platform!"

Bullets pinged against the metal frame of the platform. Anya flattened herself, holding the box tight. The platform was too slow. She looked down. Thirty floors below. She looked up. Guards were aiming again.

Suddenly, she saw an open vent on the side of the building, for air conditioning. It was only a few feet away, but the platform was moving straight down. Anya took a deep breath. She waited for the platform to align just right. Then, with a burst of speed, she leaped sideways, grabbing the edge of the vent cover. Her fingers scraped, but she held on. She pulled herself inside.

It was dark and dusty in the vent. Anya crawled fast, the sound of her knees scraping the metal echoing around her. She could hear Thorne shouting somewhere above. After a long crawl, she saw a light below. She kicked open a grate and dropped into a busy kitchen. Cooks screamed. Anya ignored them, running past them and out a back door into a dark alley.

She ran down the alley, heart pounding. A small, old car was waiting there, engine running. She jumped in. The driver sped off into the night, leaving Zenith Tower and its angry guards behind. Anya leaned back, finally letting out a long breath. She looked at the small white box in her hand. All that trouble, all that running. For this.

### Conclusion
Anya pulled up to a small house. Her younger sister, Maya, sat on the porch swing, looking sad. Anya got out of the car, walked over, and held out the box. Maya's eyes went wide. "The special Lemon Biscuits! They said they were all gone! The last box in the whole city!" Anya smiled. Maya hugged her tight. "You're the best sister ever!" Anya just patted her head. All that high-stakes action, all those guards, just for a box of her sister's favorite cookies. The company had bought the last batch, storing them in their CEO's office for a special event. But Maya's happiness was worth every single jump and scrape.
